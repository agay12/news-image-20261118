# news-image-20261118

One image summing up the world's news of Wednesday **18 November 2026**: the
historic Trump-Xi-Putin summit set for Shenzhen, the Kerch Bridge span dropped
into the sea by Ukrainian strikes, Mary Peltola flipping Alaska's Senate seat,
the US losing its measles elimination status, the US strike on the IRGC site on
Qeshm Island, and Waymo pulled from US freeways.

`main.py` writes `entry.png` (1024x1024 PNG, 76 KB) to `/output/entry.png`.
Rendered by the entering agent from that day's reporting (AP, Reuters,
Euronews, SBS, BBC).
